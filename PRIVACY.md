# NightDesk privacy policy

NightDesk is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. It has no servers and no accounts of its own, and the developer receives none of your data.

## What stays on your computer

Your subjects, study log, calendar entries, alarms, notes and images, pet, settings and backups are stored only on your PC (in `%USERPROFILE%\.nightdesk`, with backup copies in `Documents\Nightdesk Backups`). Nothing is sent anywhere unless you use one of the optional services below.

## Google Calendar (optional)

If you connect Google Calendar, NightDesk uses the Google Calendar API to read your calendars and to create, edit or delete events you choose to change in the app. Access is limited to the `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar` scope.

- Calendar data goes directly between your computer and Google. It is never sent to the developer or any third party.
- The sign-in token is stored on your PC, encrypted with Windows' own credential protection.
- You can disconnect at any time in Settings, and revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

NightDesk's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Other online features (optional)

- **Spotify**: shows what is playing and lets you control playback; talks only to Spotify.
- **Codeforces and GitHub**: reads public contest lists, your public submissions and your public contribution calendar using the handle you enter.
- **Quotes**: downloads public quotes from zenquotes.io.
